Who We Are

About Us

Circle Line Engineering Ltd is a precision engineering workshop based in Ōtāhuhu, Auckland, with over 30 years of experience delivering high-quality machined components to clients across New Zealand.

Founded and led by our experienced engineering director (NZCE, Dip. Bus.), the business has built a reputation for precision, reliability and the ability to tackle challenging jobs from prototype one-offs through to repeat production runs.

Our workshop combines CNC milling, CNC turning and conventional turning capability, giving us the flexibility to support prototypes, one-off work and repeat production across a wide range of engineering requirements.
